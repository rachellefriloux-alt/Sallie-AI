# Vision

Sallie is a personal assistant that behaves like a **trusted operator**:
- It understands your intent (conversation + context)
- It finds grounded information (your sources + optional web)
- It can take actions (tools) **only with permission**
- It learns over time (memory) in a user-visible, user-editable way

## North Star
"Sallie helps you run your life and work end-to-end — privately, safely, and with receipts."

## Who it’s for
- Individuals who want a *local-first* assistant with deep personalization
- Families who want shared-but-controlled household workflows
- Builders who want an assistant platform they can extend

## Key user promises
- **Speed**: voice and chat feel responsive
- **Truthfulness**: when unsure, say so; show evidence
- **Control**: permission gates + audit trails
- **Continuity**: remembers what you want it to remember

## What Sallie is not
- Not a single chatbot with a giant prompt
- Not "autonomy" without guardrails
- Not a promise of perfect or limitless knowledge

## Product shape
- A desktop-first experience initially (fast iteration)
- A modular platform: UI clients + core runtime + connectors
- A “Labs” layer for experimental features
